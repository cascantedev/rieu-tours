A 6 day, 5-night guided safari to four stunning parks in northern Tanzania. Tarangire, Lake Manyara, Serengeti, and Ngorongoro Crater. Starting and ending in Arusha, this tour offers diverse wildlife experiences. Witness the vast plains of Serengeti, home to the Big Five. Explore Tarangire, renowned for its elephant herds, and Ngorongoro Crater, dubbed the “garden of animals,”. Conclude your journey at the picturesque Lake Manyara, known for its flamingoes and unique tree-climbing lions.

Meal Plan: Full Board

Day 1: Arusha to Tarangire National Park

Earlier in the morning after breakfast the tour departs. First stop is Arusha Town for any last minute purchases before we head off on a safari. Afterwards we head towards the Tarangire National Park for a game drive with picnic lunch at the park. Tarangire is well known for its huge elephant population and baobab trees. It forms the center of an annual migratory cycle that includes up to 3000 elephants, 25,000 wildebeest and 30,000 zebras. Late evening after game drives you will be driven to the Lodge for Dinner and overnight.

Day 2: Tarangire to Serengeti National Park

After breakfast, depart from your lodge and journey to the world-famous Serengeti National Park, passing through the scenic highlands of Karatu and the breathtaking Ngorongoro Conservation Area. Enjoy spectacular views as you descend into the endless golden plains of the Serengeti, renowned for their incredible wildlife and natural beauty. Continue to the Seronera area, the heart of the Serengeti, famous for its rich wildlife and the Seronera River, which attracts a wide variety of animals throughout the year. Upon arrival, enjoy a delicious lunch followed by an exciting afternoon game drive, offering opportunities to spot lions, elephants, giraffes, zebras, and other fascinating wildlife in their natural habitat. Experience the magic of the African wilderness as your unforgettable safari adventure begins.

Day 3: Game Drives in Serengeti National Park

Enjoy a leisurely breakfast or embark on full-day game drives at 9:00 AM with a packed lunch, returning around 6:00 PM. Alternatively, start with a 6:00 AM game drive, then return for breakfast and relaxation before an afternoon game drive with lunch. Explore the central Serengeti, renowned for its lion and leopard populations. The Seronera area, enriched by the Seronera River, attracts diverse wildlife year-round. Look out for lion prides, especially during migration off-season. A picnic lunch is provided during the full-day drive, concluding around 5:00 PM, followed by a hearty dinner and a restful night in your accommodation.

Day 4: Game Drives in Serengeti and Transfer to Ngorongoro Conservation Area

After breakfast you will go for a half day game drives in Serengeti National Park then after Lunch you departs to Ngorongoro Conservation area for dinner and overnight. Ngorongoro is a special conservation area and not a National Park. The whole area is managed for both animals and local Maasai people who graze their cattle alongside the indigenous wildlife.

Day 5: Safari in Ngorongoro Crater and Drive Back to Arusha/Airport

Get up Earlier in the morning around 6:00am for Breakfast then descend to the crater floor for game drive. You can expect to see lions, elephants, Zebras, Hippos, Flamingos, Jackals, Rhinos Antelopes and many birds. The birds seen here include eagle, vulture, and flamingos in the Crater Lake, stork, bats, giant vulture, sacred ibis, kori bustard, blacksmith plover, long necked heron and the cattle eagle. Post the picnic lunch at the beautiful Hippo pool, you will begin a steep ascend to the top exit of the crater. With a 2hr drive left to Karatu the day will come to a close, Late afternoon you will be driven to the Lodge for Dinner and Overnight.

Day 6: Safari in Lake Manayara National Park and Drive to Arusha/Airport

After breakfast at 7:30 AM, depart for the beautiful Lake Manyara National Park for an exciting half-day game drive. Explore its lush forests, breathtaking landscapes, and diverse wildlife, including elephants, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras, baboons, and blue monkeys. Keep an eye out for the park’s famous tree-climbing lions and, depending on the season, admire the spectacular sight of flamingos along the lake shores. Enjoy a relaxing picnic lunch surrounded by nature before returning to Arusha in the afternoon. You will be dropped off at Arusha Airport or your accommodation in Arusha, marking the end of your unforgettable Tanzania safari. Depart with wonderful memories of incredible wildlife encounters, stunning landscapes, and the unique beauty of Africa.
