Enjoy your 10 days holidays in Zanzibar

Meal Plan: Full Board

Day 1: Arriving in Zanzibar

After arriving we wil pick to your Hotel.

Day 2: Stone Town Tour

After breakfast, enjoy a 3-hour guided Stone Town City Tour, visiting historic sites such as the Anglican Cathedral, former Slave Market, Sultan’s Palace, Tip Tip’s House, Old Arabic Fort, famous Zanzibar doors, and local markets. Learn about Zanzibar’s rich history and culture. Afterward, drive for 15 minutes to a Spice Plantation for a 3-hour tour. Discover Zanzibar’s spices, including cinnamon, pepper, and vanilla, learn their uses, shop for handmade products, and enjoy a traditional weaving demonstration.

Day 3: Safari Blue Tour

After breakfast, enjoy a full-day Safari Blue Tour starting from Fumba Village aboard a traditional dhow boat to explore Menai Bay. Enjoy swimming, snorkeling among colorful coral reefs, and spotting dolphins. Visit beautiful sandbanks and Kwale Island, where you will enjoy a delicious seafood lunch with grilled fish, lobster, calamari, chicken, rice, vegetables, and a selection of fresh tropical fruits.

Day 4: Trip to Nungwi Village

After breakfast departure from the hotel and transfer to North Beach Part of Zanzibar, warm welcome at DoubleTree by Hilton Hotel - NUNGWI lunch , dinner and breakfast included.

Day 5:

Day leisure or swims and play at the beach in front of the hotel, enjoying the beach or flexible to arrange any activities.

Day 6: Dolphin Tour and Jozani forest Adventure

After breakfast, visit Kizimkazi for an exciting dolphin tour, with opportunities to spot dolphins, swim, and snorkel. Visit the historic 12th-century mosque, then explore Jozani Forest to see Zanzibar’s unique red colobus monkeys, indigenous trees, and beautiful mangrove forests. After the tours, return to the hotel for lunch, dinner, and overnight stay.

Day 7: Leisure and Beach activities

Day leisure or swims and play at the beach in front of the hotel, enjoying the beach or be flexible to arrange any activities

Day 8: Mnemba Island Snorkeling and Dolphin Tour

Morning departure from your hotels identical to a full day, but at lunch time we anchor pristine ATOLL OF MNEMBA ISLAND and will set up a delicious meal of lobster, shrimp, squid, octopus, fresh fruits, water and drinks, and small snacks. MNEMBA ISLAND is a protected natural park, where you can admire corals and tropical fish that many of us have only seen in an aquarium, sea turtles and dolphins. It is famous for encounters with green turtles and common Dolphins. For some reason Mnemba dolphins really like to play. They often tend to swim right next to our boat, and so allow you to jump in the water to snorkel with them. After tours, you will be dropped off at your hotel for Lunch/dinner and overnight.

Day 9: Leisure and Beach activities

Day leisure or swims and play at the beach in front of the hotel, enjoying the beach or be flexible to arrange any activities.

Day 10:Departure Day

Breakfast at the hotel - some leisure around the hotel till the time for departure, we will drive you to the airport/seaport.
