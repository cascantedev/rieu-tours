About Us

We will take you to the top destinations in Tanzania mainland and Zanzibar island.

We are a Zanzibar based tour operator specialized in arranging fully guided Tanzania safaris and beach holidays on the spice islands of Zanzibar, Mafia and Pemba, for individuals and small groups of relaxed, easygoing family or friends who want to travel at a more relaxed pace and visit some of the more unusual places and people in Zanzibar and Tanzania.
