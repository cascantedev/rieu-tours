Safari Blue is widely considered Zanzibar’s premier full-day sea adventure, offering an unforgettable journey into the pristine marine environment of the Menai Bay Conservation Area. The tour officially begins from the fishing village of Fumba, where guests board a traditional, hand-crafted wooden dhow boat equipped with canvas sails to experience authentic Swahili seafaring

Meal Plan: Lunch

1: Hotel pick-up

We pick you up from your hotel and drive to Fumba Fishing Village, where your ocean adventure begins.

2: Blue Lagoon Snorkeling

Discover colorful tropical fish such as Nemo, Dory, zebra fish, and many more. Enjoy snorkeling in crystal-clear waters and relax on a beautiful white sandy sandbank

3: Delicious Seafood Lunch

: Sail to Kwale Island and enjoy a freshly prepared seafood feast, including lobster, prawns, octopus, fish, calamari, and chips, cooked by our professional chef.

4: Historic Baobab Tree

Explore the famous giant baobab tree and capture beautiful photos to remember your Zanzibar adventure.

5: Return to Your Hotel

: After an amazing day at sea, we take you back to Fumba, where your transport will be waiting to return you to your hotel.

✨ Swim, snorkel, taste fresh seafood, and discover the natural beauty of Zanzibar—all in one incredible experience!
