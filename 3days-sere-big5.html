This short Budget Serengeti and Ngorongoro Safari Guided offers you the rare chance to visit this world celebrated national park, for quite some short time, starting from Arusha, the safari capital and base town of safaris in Northern Tanzania you will have 1 night in Central Serengeti and 1 night Ngorongoro Crater, Tour starts and ends at Arusha.

Meal Plan: Full Board

Day 1: Arusha – Serengeti National Park

Your safari starts after breakfast with a hotel pickup in Arusha. Head to Serengeti National Park, covering 240 km in about 6hrs. The Serengeti, meaning "endless plains," spans 14,763 Sq Kms and hosts the "Big 5." It supports Africa's greatest concentration of plains game. Seronera, the central area, boasts rich wildlife, especially lions and leopards. Look out for the granite "Kopjes," offering shelter to diverse flora and fauna. Enjoy a picnic lunch during the game drive. The day ends with a hearty dinner and rest at your accommodation.

Day 2: Game Drives in Central Serengeti and Drive to Ngorongoro

Begin your day with an optional early morning game drive at sunrise, offering a wonderful opportunity to witness wildlife as the Serengeti awakens. After breakfast at your lodge or camp, depart with a packed lunch for another exciting game drive across the endless plains of Serengeti National Park.

Explore the breathtaking landscapes of this world-famous wilderness, home to an incredible diversity of wildlife and the spectacular Great Wildebeest Migration, where millions of wildebeests, zebras, and gazelles move across the Serengeti ecosystem in search of fresh grazing and water. In the late afternoon, depart for the magnificent Ngorongoro Crater rim, where you will enjoy dinner, relax, and spend the night surrounded by the beautiful scenery of Tanzania’s highlands.

Day 3: Ngorongoro Crater Tour Transfer to Arusha

Begin your final safari day with an early breakfast before descending into the magnificent Ngorongoro Crater at around 6:30 AM for an unforgettable game drive. Explore this remarkable natural wonder, renowned for its breathtaking landscapes and abundant wildlife, including elephants, buffaloes, lions, hyenas, hippos, and the rare black rhino.

Enjoy an exciting game drive across the crater floor, capturing incredible wildlife moments and discovering the beauty of this unique ecosystem. Take a relaxing picnic lunch near the scenic Hippo Pool before ascending the crater walls and beginning your journey back to Arusha. Upon arrival, you will be dropped off at Arusha Airport or your preferred location in Arusha, depending on your flight schedule. As your safari comes to an end, depart with unforgettable memories of Tanzania’s spectacular wildlife, stunning landscapes, and extraordinary African wilderness.
