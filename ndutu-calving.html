This 7 Days 6 Nights Tanzania Guided Safari gives an opportunity to see Great Wildebeest Migration. Over 2 million Wildebeest, Zebra and Gazelles move through the Serengeti and Masai Mara ecosystems in search of green pastures and safe calving ground. Their pattern of movement is highly weather dependent and dynamic, from November to March the Herds of Migrants are in Southern Serengeti where they give Birth. Apart from tracking the migration you will discover other Parks too, where you can witness resident game, provide you with an unforgettable experience, this safari can be done only as a private safari. The choice of accommodation available is mid-range to luxury grade tented camps and lodges.

Meal Plan: Full Board

Day 1: Arusha – Tarangire National Park

Your safari guide will pick you up from your hotel in Arusha at 8:00 AM for a 2–3-hour drive to Tarangire National Park. Enjoy a full-day game drive through diverse landscapes, spotting giant baobab trees, elephants, giraffes, lions, zebras, and other wildlife. A picnic lunch will be served during the tour. In the late afternoon, return to your camp or lodge for dinner and an overnight stay.

Accommodation: Farm of Dreams Lodge

Meals: Lunch and Dinner

Day 2: Tarangire to Southern Serengeti Ndutu Area

After breakfast, depart from Karatu and drive for about 3 hours through the Ngorongoro Conservation Area to Ndutu in Southern Serengeti. Enjoy a game drive with a picnic lunch, exploring vast grasslands that host the Great Migration of wildebeest, zebras, and gazelles, especially between December and April during the calving season. Ndutu is also home to predators such as lions, leopards, cheetahs, and other wild cats. Enjoy wildlife viewing around Lake Ndutu and Lake Masek before ending your game drive around 5:00 PM. Proceed to your chosen accommodation for dinner and an overnight stay.

Day 3: Full Day Safari in Southern Serengeti Ndutu area

After breakfast, depart for a full-day game drive across the endless plains of Southern Serengeti and the Ndutu region, exploring the Serengeti National Park and Ngorongoro Conservation Area. Enjoy another opportunity to track the Great Migration depending on its location. Between December and April, witness wildebeest calving and observe predators hunting their vulnerable young, offering an unforgettable wildlife experience. Around 5:00 PM, return to your accommodation for a freshly prepared dinner and an overnight stay.

Accommodation: Sero Tented Camp

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Day 4: Game Drives in Ndutu Southern Serengeti to Central Serengeti

After breakfast, enjoy a full-day game drive from Southern to Central Serengeti, exploring the endless grasslands and breathtaking landscapes. After lunch, continue towards Central Serengeti National Park, famous for its vast plains and abundant wildlife.

Look out for the Big Five (elephant, rhino, buffalo, lion, and leopard), as well as impalas and other wildlife. Discover the unique granite rock formations known as kopjes, which provide shelter for various animals. In the evening, head to your accommodation for dinner and an overnight stay.

Accommodation: Serengeti Heritage Luxury Camp

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Day 5: Full Day Game Drives in Central Serengeti National Park

After breakfast at the lodge, enjoy a full-day game drive in Central Serengeti, particularly the Seronera area, known for its rich wildlife and large populations of lions and leopards. The Seronera River attracts diverse animals, offering excellent wildlife viewing throughout the year

Enjoy a picnic lunch during the game drive as you explore the park and observe wildlife, including lion prides and other fascinating species. Around 5:00 PM, return to your accommodation for dinner and an overnight stay.

Accommodation: Serengeti Heritage Luxury Camp

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Day 6: Half Game Drives in Serengeti and Transfer to Ngorongoro Area

After breakfast you will go for a half day game drives in Serengeti National Park then after Lunch you departs to Ngorongoro Conservation area for dinner and overnight. Ngorongoro is a special conservation area and not a National Park. The whole area is managed for both animals and local Maasai people who graze their cattle alongside the indigenous wildlife.

Accommodation: Ang’ata Ngorongoro Camp

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Day 7: Safari in Ngorongoro Crater to Arusha

Get up Earlier in the morning around 6:00am for Breakfast then descend to the crater floor for game drive. You can expect to see lions, elephants, Zebras, Hippos, Flamingos, Jackals, Rhinos Antelopes and many birds. The birds seen here include eagle, vulture, and flamingos in the Crater Lake, stork, bats, giant vulture, sacred ibis, kori bustard, blacksmith plover, long necked heron and the cattle eagle. Post the picnic lunch at the beautiful Hippo pool, you will begin a steep ascend to the top exit of the crater. With a 2hr drive left to Karatu the day will come to a close, Late afternoon you will be driven back to Arusha where you will be dropped off at Arusha Airport or your Hotel in Arusha.

Accommodation: None on Request

Meals: Breakfast and Lunch
