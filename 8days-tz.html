Experience 8-day safaris in Tanzania, exploring Serengeti, Tarangire, Ngorongoro Crater, Lake Manyara, and Arusha National Park. Ideal for families with kids, this relaxed tour offers ample time to encounter iconic wildlife such as lions, leopards, elephants, rhinos, and cape buffalo. Discover Ngorongoro Crater and Serengeti National Park, witnessing vast herds of wildebeest, zebra, giraffes, hippos, cheetahs, hyenas, and more.

Meal Plan: Full Board

Day 1: Arival Day

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) your Tour Guide will pick you and transferred to Arusha to recover from your journey and, if you’re lucky, to catch a glimpse of the spectacular Kilimanjaro which rises from the clouds above town. When you get to your Hotel you will be briefed about your safari then the rest of your Day will be at Leisure.

Day 2: Arusha to Tarangire National park

Earlier in the morning after breakfast the tour departs. First stop is Arusha Town for any last minute purchases before we head off on a safari. Afterwards we head towards the Tarangire National Park for a game drive with picnic lunch at the park. Tarangire is well known for its huge elephant population and baobab trees. It forms the center of an annual migratory cycle that includes up to 3000 elephants, 25,000 wildebeest and 30,000 zebras. Late evening after game drives you will be driven to the Lodge for Dinner and overnight.

Day 3:Tarangire to Serengeti National Park

After breakfast, we depart from the lodge and drive to the world-famous Serengeti National Park, passing through the scenic highlands of Karatu and the spectacular Ngorongoro Conservation Area. As we descend into the vast plains of the Serengeti, enjoy breathtaking landscapes and the beauty of Africa’s untouched wilderness.

We proceed to the Seronera area, the heart of the Serengeti, renowned for its rich wildlife and the life-giving Seronera River, which attracts a wide variety of animals. Upon arrival, we enjoy lunch followed by an exciting afternoon game drive, offering opportunities to spot lions, elephants, giraffes, zebras, and many other fascinating wildlife species in their natural habitat

Day 4:Game Drives in Serengeti National Park

After breakfast, enjoy an unforgettable wildlife adventure in the heart of Serengeti National Park. Choose between a full-day game drive with a picnic lunch or an early morning game drive followed by breakfast and an afternoon wildlife excursion.

Explore the famous Seronera Valley, renowned for its abundant wildlife, especially lions, leopards, elephants, giraffes, and other fascinating species. The Seronera River provides a vital water source, attracting a diverse range of animals throughout the year, making this area ideal for exceptional wildlife viewing. After an exciting day of exploration, return to your accommodation in the late afternoon to enjoy a delicious dinner, relax, and reflect on the unforgettable experiences of the African wilderness.

Day 5:Game Drive in Serengeti and Transfer to Ngorongoro Conservation Area

After breakfast you will go for a half day game drives in Serengeti National Park then after Lunch you departs to Ngorongoro Conservation area for dinner and overnight. Ngorongoro is a special conservation area and not a National Park. The whole area is managed for both animals and local Maasai people who graze their cattle alongside the indigenous wildlife.

Day 6:Ngorongoro Crater to Karatu

Early in the morning, around 6:00 AM, enjoy breakfast before descending into the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater for an unforgettable game drive. Explore this remarkable wildlife paradise, home to lions, elephants, zebras, hippos, flamingos, and the rare black rhino, alongside a variety of fascinating bird species.

Enjoy a scenic picnic lunch near the beautiful Hippo Pool, surrounded by breathtaking landscapes and the wonders of nature. After lunch, ascend the crater walls and drive to Karatu, where you will arrive at your lodge in the late afternoon. Relax, enjoy a delicious dinner, and spend a peaceful night reflecting on the incredible wildlife experiences of the day.

Day 7:Lake Manyara National Park to Arusha

After breakfast at 7:30 AM, depart for the beautiful Lake Manyara National Park for an exciting half-day game drive. Discover its lush forests, scenic landscapes, and diverse wildlife, including elephants, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras, baboons, and blue monkeys. Keep an eye out for the park’s famous tree-climbing lions and, depending on the season, enjoy the spectacular sight of flamingos along the lake shores.

Enjoy a relaxing picnic lunch surrounded by nature before departing for Arusha in the afternoon. Upon arrival, you will be dropped off at Planet Lodge Arusha or a similar accommodation, marking the end of your unforgettable Tanzania safari adventure filled with incredible wildlife encounters and lasting memories.

Day 8:Materuni Waterfalls and Chemka Hot spring Tour

After breakfast, embark on an unforgettable cultural and nature experience with our professional English-speaking guide to Materuni Village, home of the Chagga people. Enjoy a scenic walk to the magnificent Materuni Waterfalls, surrounded by lush greenery and breathtaking mountain views. Learn about local traditions, village life, and the rich culture of the Chagga community before enjoying a refreshing swim and a delicious traditional lunch.

Continue with an authentic coffee tour, where you will discover the traditional process of growing, roasting, and grinding coffee beans while enjoying lively Chagga songs and dances. Savor a freshly brewed cup of locally produced coffee before heading to the beautiful Kikuletwa Hot Springs for relaxation, swimming, and a refreshing escape into nature.

In the afternoon, depart for Kilimanjaro International Airport for your onward journey, taking home unforgettable memories of Tanzania’s natural beauty, rich culture, and warm hospitality.
