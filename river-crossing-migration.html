In September, the wildebeest herds are spread out over the northern Serengeti National Park and Kenya’s Masai Mara Game Reserve. Several large herds of wildebeest remain behind in the area and weather changes may sometimes bring the herds back into the area from the Mara. In October, the herds move south towards the central Serengeti National Park via the Lobo area of the Serengeti National Park.

Meal Plan: Full Board

Day 1: Arusha to Tarangire National Park

Your professional safari guide will pick you up from your hotel in Arusha at 8:00 AM for a 2–3 hour drive to Tarangire National Park. Enjoy a full-day game drive through beautiful landscapes, giant baobab trees, and diverse wildlife, including elephants, giraffes, lions, zebras, and antelopes. You will also enjoy a picnic lunch inside the park. In the late afternoon, return to your camp or lodge for a delicious dinner and a relaxing overnight stay.

Accommodation: Farm of Dreams Lodge Meals: Lunch and Dinner.

Day 2: Tarangire Area to Serengeti National park

After breakfast, drive to Serengeti National Park through the scenic Karatu highlands and Ngorongoro Conservation Area. Explore the endless plains and enjoy a game drive in the Seronera area, famous for its rich wildlife and the Seronera River. Arrive in time for lunch, followed by an afternoon game drive to spot lions, elephants, giraffes, and other wildlife.

Accommodation: Serengeti Heritage Luxury Camp

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner

Day 3:Central Serengeti to North Serengeti

After breakfast, enjoy a morning game drive in central Serengeti as you travel towards the northern region of Kogatende. Explore the changing landscapes and spot wildlife such as zebras, gazelles, buffaloes, leopards, and lions. Enjoy a picnic lunch along the way and, depending on arrival time, visit the famous Mara River. In the evening, relax at your camp and enjoy dinner.

Accommodation: Sero Tented Camp

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner

Day 4:Full Game Drive In Northern Serengeti

After breakfast, enjoy a full-day game drive in Northern Serengeti towards the famous Mara River, known for the Great Wildebeest Migration river crossings, especially from mid-June to September. Look out for wildebeest, zebras, crocodiles, lions, and other wildlife. Depending on the migration, you may also explore the Lobo Valley. In the evening, return to camp for dinner and an overnight stay.

Accommodation: Sero Tented Camp

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner

Day 5:North Serengeti To Central Serengeti

After breakfast, enjoy a half-day game drive in Northern Serengeti before travelling south to the central Serengeti (Seronera area). Enjoy scenic landscapes and wildlife viewing along the way, including lions, elephants, and other animals. Seronera is famous for its rich wildlife and numerous lion prides. Enjoy a picnic lunch during the journey, followed by dinner and an overnight stay at your accommodation. The next day, continue to the Ngorongoro Conservation Area.

Day 6:North Serengeti to Ngorongoro Conservation Area

After breakfast, enjoy a full-day game drive in Northern Serengeti, exploring the famous Mara River and Lobo Valley. From mid-June to September, you may witness the Great Wildebeest Migration river crossings. Look out for lions, leopards, cheetahs, and other wildlife while enjoying the beautiful landscapes. After a picnic lunch, drive through Central Serengeti towards the Ngorongoro Conservation Area for dinner and an overnight stay.

Accommodation: Ang’ata Ngorongoro Camp

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner

Day 7:Safari in Ngorongoro Crater to Karatu

After an early breakfast at 6:00 AM, descend into the Ngorongoro Crater for a game drive. Spot wildlife such as lions, elephants, zebras, hippos, flamingos, antelopes, and possibly the endangered black rhino. Enjoy a picnic lunch at the beautiful Hippo Pool before ascending the crater and driving to Karatu for dinner and an overnight stay.

Accommodation: Farm of Dreams Lodge

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner

Day 8:Game Driive in Lakee Manyara National Park and Drive to Arusha

After breakfast at 7:30 AM, depart for Lake Manyara National Park for a half-day game drive. Explore lush forests and look out for elephants, giraffes, buffaloes, baboons, blue monkeys, and possibly tree-climbing lions. Depending on the season, you may also see flamingos along the lake. Enjoy a picnic lunch before driving back to Arusha, where you will be dropped off at the airport or your accommodation.

Accommodation: None (on request)

Meals: Breakfast and Lunch
