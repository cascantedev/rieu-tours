Experience an unforgettable 2-day Tanzania safari exploring two of the country’s most remarkable wildlife destinations: Tarangire National Park and the magnificent Ngorongoro Crater, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Discover Tarangire, famous for its ancient baobab trees, large herds of elephants, and diverse wildlife roaming across its beautiful savannah landscapes. Continue to the breathtaking Ngorongoro Crater, home to an extraordinary concentration of wildlife, including lions, elephants, zebras, buffaloes, and the rare black rhino. With comfortable accommodation in Karatu or the surrounding areas, this short safari offers an incredible opportunity to experience Tanzania’s natural beauty, spectacular scenery, and rich wildlife in just two days and one night. It is the perfect adventure for travelers seeking an authentic African safari within a limited time.

Meal Plan: Full Board

Day 1: Arusha – Tarangire National Park

After breakfast, you will be picked up from Arusha Airport or your hotel in Arusha and depart for the magnificent Tarangire National Park with a picnic lunch. Famous for its ancient baobab trees, scenic landscapes, and large herds of elephants, Tarangire offers an unforgettable introduction to Tanzania’s incredible wildlife.

Enjoy an exciting game drive along the Tarangire River, where you may encounter elephants, lions, leopards, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, wildebeests, and various antelope species. Keep your camera ready to capture the beauty of the African wilderness and its remarkable wildlife. In the late afternoon, drive to your lodge or camp, where a delicious dinner and a relaxing overnight stay await, surrounded by the peaceful beauty of Tanzania’s natural landscapes.

Day 2: Ngorongoro Crater – Arusha

After an early breakfast, depart for the magnificent Ngorongoro Crater for an unforgettable game drive across its spectacular crater floor. This UNESCO World Heritage Site is renowned for its breathtaking landscapes and remarkable concentration of wildlife, including elephants, buffaloes, lions, hyenas, hippos, and the rare black rhino.

Enjoy an exciting game drive with opportunities to witness wildlife in its natural habitat and capture incredible photographs. Later, stop for a relaxing picnic lunch near the beautiful Hippo Pool before ascending the crater walls and beginning your journey back to Arusha. Upon arrival, you will be dropped off at Arusha Airport or your preferred location in Arusha, depending on your flight schedule. As your safari comes to an end, depart with unforgettable memories of Tanzania’s spectacular wildlife, stunning scenery, and extraordinary African wilderness.
