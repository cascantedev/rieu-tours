Experience aJoin on a thrilling 4-day, 3-night Serengeti and Ngorongoro adventure in northern Tanzania. Explore the vast plains of Serengeti National Park, known for its diverse landscapes and abundant wildlife. From grassland plains to hilly terrain, valleys, rivers, and forests, the Serengeti offers untamed wilderness teeming with safari animals. Then, journey to the Ngorongoro Crater, where the chance to spot the Big Five in a day awaits in this natural wonderland known as the garden of animals .

Meal Plan: Full Board

Day 1: Arusha – Serengeti National Park

After breakfast, you will be picked up from your hotel or lodge in Arusha and begin your exciting journey to the world-famous Serengeti National Park. Travel through the scenic highlands of Karatu and the magnificent Ngorongoro Conservation Area, enjoying breathtaking landscapes along the way before descending into the endless golden plains of the Serengeti. Proceed to the Seronera area, the heart of the Serengeti, renowned for its abundant wildlife and the Seronera River, which attracts a wide variety of animals throughout the year. Upon arrival, enjoy a delicious lunch followed by an exciting afternoon game drive, offering opportunities to spot lions, elephants, giraffes, zebras, and other fascinating wildlife in their natural habitat. Experience the beauty of Africa’s wilderness and create unforgettable memories on your first day of safari.

Day 2: Game Drives in Serengeti National Park

Enjoy a full day of unforgettable wildlife adventures in the heart of Serengeti National Park. Choose between an early morning game drive at 6:00 AM or a full-day safari starting at 9:00 AM, with a delicious picnic lunch in the wilderness. Explore the famous Seronera Valley, renowned for its abundant wildlife, especially lions, leopards, elephants, giraffes, and zebras. The Seronera River provides a vital water source, attracting a diverse range of animals throughout the year and offering exceptional wildlife-viewing opportunities. After an exciting day of exploration across the vast savannah, return to your accommodation in the late afternoon. Enjoy a delicious dinner, relax in the peaceful surroundings, and reflect on the remarkable wildlife encounters and unforgettable memories of your Serengeti safari.

Day 3: Game Drives in Serengeti and Transfer to Ngorongoro Conservation Area

After breakfast you will go for a half day game drives in Serengeti National Park then after Lunch you departs to Ngorongoro Conservation area for dinner and overnight. Ngorongoro is a special conservation area and not a National Park. The whole area is managed for both animals and local Maasai people who graze their cattle alongside the indigenous wildlife.

Day 4: Safari in Ngorongoro Crater and Drive Back to Arusha/Airport

Early in the morning, enjoy breakfast before descending into the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater for an unforgettable game drive. Explore this remarkable wildlife paradise, home to lions, elephants, zebras, hippos, flamingos, antelopes, and the rare black rhino, alongside a variety of fascinating bird species. Enjoy a scenic picnic lunch near the beautiful Hippo Pool, surrounded by breathtaking landscapes and incredible wildlife. After lunch, ascend the crater walls and begin your journey back to Arusha, enjoying the scenic views along the way. Upon arrival, you will be dropped off at Arusha Airport or your hotel in Arusha, marking the end of your incredible Tanzania safari filled with unforgettable wildlife encounters and lasting memories.
